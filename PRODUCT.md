## What it is
Leoz Gents Salon — a premium men's grooming salon in Dubai (two branches: Palm Jumeirah &
Sky Gardens), operating since 2012. Full gents salon: haircuts & shaving, facial & skin,
massage & body, mani/pedi. Booking via Fresha. This project is a **concept website** built by
the Propagenda agency to (a) win the rebuild engagement and (b) show Leoz a site worthy of the brand.

## Unique mechanism
The royal treatment — the lion and the crown made literal (gold lion mark, crowned-lion statues
in the salon, the "Royal Shave"). "A king's grooming." Traditional technique + modern luxury,
for "the modern gentleman who demands excellence."

## Audience & scene
Affluent men in Dubai choosing a premium grooming home; mostly on a phone, often deciding to book
now or walk in (open till midnight). The site must read premium instantly and make booking one tap.

## This surface (Persuade — concept homepage)
Success = a first-time visitor instantly understands this is Dubai's premium gents salon, feels the
quality, and books (Fresha) or walks in. Secondary: the agency's taste is evident to the client.

## Proof / content available (real)
Professional photo shoot (interiors, illuminated lion signage, gold crowned-lions, craft & hospitality
details); the real price list (AED); their company-profile copy & values; a Google review
(Alaa Taifor); two branches, hours 10:00–24:00; Fresha booking; IG @leoz_saloon; WhatsApp.

## Constraints
- Real Leoz assets only (no stock). EN now; AR is a later phase (keep layout RTL-friendly).
- Mobile-first, no horizontal scroll, accessible.
- Static single-page concept (poc/index.html); not the production React app, not the quote.

## Brand commitments (durable)
- Name on site: **LEOZ** wordmark + "GENTS SALON". Gold-on-dark. The lion & crown motif.
- Gold (#d8b25a) is the only accent. Dark/cinematic use-scene (the salon is dark + warm).
- Booking provider: Fresha (external), styled to the site, two-branch picker.
