# Leoz — Creative Direction (locked north star)

Decided with the client 2026-10-16. This is the brief the concept hero/site builds from.

## The chosen three references (client-picked top 3)
1. **Louis XIII Cognac** (louisxiii-cognac.com) → THE RITUAL. Slow, reverent, gold-on-black
   restraint; the act made a ceremony; generous negative space; cinematic hush. Not busy.
2. **Truefitt & Hill** (truefittandhill.co.uk) → THE HERITAGE. Royal-warrant gravitas; the
   crown & lion; "grooming men for greatness"; old-world authority and trust.
3. **Barbury / jetmirs.ch** → THE STRUCTURE. The working dark-barber site: full-bleed hero,
   oversized wordmark, walk-in + Book duality, named signature packages.

## Synthesis = Direction B (The Lion), refined
Barbury's dark-cinematic barber **structure**, executed with Louis XIII's ritual
**restraint** and Truefitt's royal-heritage **gravitas** — on Leoz's own gold-on-black
lion-and-crown brand. Concept line: **"A king's grooming."**

## The recipe
- **Palette:** near-black #0a0a0b / charcoal #16151a base · gold #d8b25a + gold-lite #ecd79b
  accent · white #f4f1ec text · material accents sparingly (green leather, burnt-orange, marble).
  Gold is the ONLY accent. High contrast, lots of black space (Louis XIII restraint).
- **Type:** oversized uppercase grotesk wordmark **LEOZ** (Barbury scale) · engraved serif
  (Cinzel) for the heritage/ritual lines and section flourishes (Truefitt/their "GENTS SALON")
  · clean geometric sans (Poppins) body · the gold corner-bracket motif from their print.
  EN pairs with a matching Arabic display face for AR.
- **Motion:** slow, reverent reveals; full-bleed hero video from their REAL reels (15+ in Drive);
  no bounce, no clutter — restraint over flash.
- **Photography:** their real shoot only (dark, warm, premium). Macro on the gold tools
  (Jaeger-LeCoultre-style) for craft detail. Hero portraits from the full DSC088xx set.
- **Tone / copy:** reverent, regal, confident. Their own words — "redefine men's grooming…
  luxury, precision, individualized care… transform routine grooming into a truly indulgent
  experience." Frame the royal shave as a rite with heritage.
- **Structure (Barbury):** full-bleed cinematic hero (LEOZ + "Est. 2012" + Book + walk-ins) →
  story → services as named signature packages → the ritual → real price list → gallery →
  Google reviews / press → two-branch locations (Palm Jumeirah / Sky Gardens) w/ Fresha deep-link → footer.

## Booking
Fresha, styled to the site, one tap away, with a two-branch picker (Palm Jumeirah / Sky Gardens).
