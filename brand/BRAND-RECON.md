# Leo'z Barber — brand reconnaissance (from their own site + assets)

Source: https://leozbarber.com/ (fetched 2026-10-16). Rung 1 — observed directly.

## Identity (note: naming is inconsistent on their own site — CONFIRM with client)
- Domain / page title: **LEOZ BARBER**. Desk wordmark: **LeoZ** (casual script).
- Footer / wordmark logo: **LEOZ GENTS SALON**. Body copy also says "LEO'S SALOON", "Sublime Barbershop".
- Working name until confirmed: **Leo'z Barber** (the domain).

## Logo & marks (saved in brand/site-grab/)
- Primary mark: **gold-gradient lion head** (Leo = lion). Strong, ownable asset.
- Wordmark: white knockout "…GENTS SALON" — built for dark backgrounds.
- => Their own system is **dark + gold**. Not an imposed choice.

## Real space (photo 2025_01_Leoz.jpeg) — the truth of the brand
Modern **Gulf luxe**, NOT rustic/heritage barbershop:
- Charcoal/near-black ceiling + walls, grey **marble** reception desk, warm **wood** veneer.
- Warm **amber** pendant lighting (woven rattan), linear LED ceiling strips, backlit marble mirrors.
- Black leather barber chairs, burnt-orange leather sofa accent.
- **Gold crowned-lion statues** on the desk — the royal/king motif is physically in the shop.

## Organizing idea (the throughline for the concept)
**The lion · the crown · the royal treatment — "a king's grooming."**
- Reinforced by: the lion mark, the gold crowned lions, and their hero service **"Royal Shave"**.
- Palette (grounded in the real space): near-black + charcoal, cool grey stone, warm wood,
  **gold** accent, amber glow. B&W editorial photography + gold is a safe, premium lane.
