What the brand already is

The brand truth

We're not inventing a look — Leoz already has a strong one, running across their salon, signage, print and social. Our job is to carry it to the web and elevate it. The organizing idea is already theirs: the lion · the crown · the royal treatment.

  • Identity Gold lion mark (Leo = lion) + "Leoz / GENTS SALON" lockup. The crowned-lion statue is in the salon.
  • The space Charcoal + grey marble + warm wood, amber light, green leather, burnt-orange accents, backlit onyx mirrors.
  • Positioning "Redefining men's grooming — luxury, precision, individualized care, for the modern gentleman."
  • Signature The hot-towel / royal shave ritual — "routine grooming into a truly indulgent experience."
  • Since 2012 · two Dubai branches · booking on Fresha (live).
  • Today's site A WordPress/Elementor template — far below the brand. That's the gap we close.

Thinking outside the niche

Luxury houses, not barbershops

Dark + gold + regal isn't a barbershop language — it's a luxury-maison language. These six (cognac, whisky, motorcars, fragrance, watchmaking, grooming) show how to make Leoz feel like a house, not a salon template — and each hands us one specific move.

Louis XIII Cognac
Steal: a simple act made sacred — the ritual treatment + absolute gold-on-black restraint + a heritage-of-time story. → make the royal shave feel like a rite, not a haircut.
louisxiii-cognac.com ↗
The Macallan Whisky
Steal: immersive scroll storytelling of craft, warm dark + gold, cinematic chapters. → tell the craft of the cut & shave the same way.
themacallan.com ↗
Rolls-Royce Motorcars
Steal: bespoke, made-for-you framing + a regal figurehead (Spirit of Ecstasy). → Leoz's lion as the figurehead; "bespoke grooming."
rolls-roycemotorcars.com ↗
Amouage Fragrance · Oman
Steal: Arabian luxury codes in gold — opulent yet tasteful, built for a bilingual world. → the exact cultural register for a Dubai EN/AR site.
amouage.com ↗
Jaeger-LeCoultre Watchmaking
Steal: precision & craftsmanship storytelling, macro detail on metal. → mirrors Leoz's "precision" value and their gold tools.
jaeger-lecoultre.com ↗
Tom Ford Grooming
Steal: black + gold, masculine, editorial — sexy and confident. → the closest actual palette to Leoz among these, in a grooming context.
tomfordbeauty.com ↗

The through-line: all six take something ordinary and frame it as a rite with heritage. That's the move for the royal shave — and it's native to Leoz's lion-and-crown world.
